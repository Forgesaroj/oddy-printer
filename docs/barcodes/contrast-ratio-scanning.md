# Print Contrast Signal (PCS) and Reflectance Standards

## Optical Reflectance and Contrast Standards

The international standard for barcode readability is defined by the Print Contrast Signal (PCS):
$$\text{PCS} = \frac{R_L - R_D}{R_L}$$
Where:
- $R_L$ = Reflectance of the background substrate (light area).
- $R_D$ = Reflectance of the printed bar (dark area).

### Quality Thresholds
- **Minimum PCS for Retail POS:** $\ge 0.70$ (70% contrast).
- **Substrate Whiteness:** Oddy sticker paper has a brightness rating of $>90\text{ CIE}$, providing an excellent $R_L \approx 0.88$.
- **Toner Density:** Black toner must reach an optical density of $>1.2\text{ OD}$ for guaranteed Grade-A barcode verification.
