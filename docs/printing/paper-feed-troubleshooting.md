# Paper Transport & Roller Skew Troubleshooting

## Resolving Paper Transport and Skew Issues

Adhesive sheets have higher caliper stiffness (backing paper + glue layer + facestock = ~150–175 gsm) compared to standard 75 gsm office paper.

### Common Failure Modes & Solutions

1. **Multi-Sheet Feeding (Double Pick):**
   - *Cause:* Static electricity between smooth release backing liners.
   - *Solution:* Fan the ream vigorously before inserting. Flex the stack gently in an S-curve to break electrostatic vacuum bonds between sheets.

2. **Rotational Skew:**
   - *Cause:* Paper guide flaps in the tray are loose, allowing the sheet to twist 1°–2° as the pick roller engages.
   - *Solution:* Push the side guide rails snugly against the sheet edges without buckling the paper.

3. **Toner Flaking on Borders:**
   - *Cause:* Heavy paper draws heat away from the fuser roller faster than normal paper.
   - *Solution:* Switch printer driver setting to **"Thick Paper 2 / Labels"**. This reduces RPM and increases fuser dwell time.
