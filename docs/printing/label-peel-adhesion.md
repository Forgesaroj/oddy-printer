# Thermal Curing and Surface Adhesion Guidelines

## Thermal Curing, Settling Time, and Surface Application

### Post-Print Settling Period
Freshly printed laser sticker sheets emerge from the output tray at temperatures exceeding 60°C.
- Allow sheets to sit flat on a table for **at least 3 to 5 minutes** before peeling.
- Attempting to peel hot stickers can cause the hot-melt acrylic adhesive to shear unevenly, leaving gummy residue on the liner.

### Substrate Surface Preparation
For maximum adhesive bonding:
1. **Clean Surfaces:** Wipe plastic, metal, or corrugated cartons clean of industrial oils and cardboard dust.
2. **Application Temperature:** Apply at temperatures above 10°C (50°F).
3. **Firm Pressure:** Oddy stickers use Pressure-Sensitive Adhesives (PSA). Rub firmly from center to edges with a thumb or rubber roller to activate full micro-pore bonding.
